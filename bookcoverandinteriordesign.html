Book cover design and interior formatting for Amazon KDP

Editing is my main work, but a well-edited book still needs a cover and pages that look professional. I design both, so the promise on the cover matches what is inside.

Get your free sample editSee my work

Cover design

  • Front, spine and back designed as one wrap
  • Print specs set for your trim size, bleed and spine width
  • Space left for the barcode
  • Titles and author names that stay readable at thumbnail size
  • Mockups you can use for launch posts and sales pages

Interior formatting

  • Title page, contents, author’s note and chapter openers
  • Page numbers: roman for front matter, then arabic
  • Layouts for Kindle eBooks, paperbacks and hardcovers on Amazon KDP
  • Bilingual layouts, with each text block placed beside its illustration

Why editing and design from one person

Nothing gets lost in a handoff. Where a contents page or a copyright line needs a decision, I flag it as a question while I am formatting, not after the book is printed.

Examples

The Book of Eno: a Kindle cover built to read at thumbnail size, with an interior of six page types. El Cocodrilo Kiko: an 80-page bilingual hardcover. Finding My Direction: a plain Word file turned into a designed interior with part pages and illustrated chapter openers.

Questions about this service

Which platforms do you prepare files for?
Amazon KDP, for Kindle eBooks, paperbacks and hardcovers. Tell me the formats you need when you ask for a quote.
Can you design from my own idea?
Yes. For The Book of Eno, I designed the Kindle cover with the author, starting from his concept: Eno, the old car and the church.
